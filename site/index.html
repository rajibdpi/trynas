A SMALL APP. A CAPABLE HOME.

Everything in its place.

Manage your files, share on your terms, and keep an eye on the machine behind it all.

01 / ORGANIZE

A better view of your files.

Browse, search, upload, download, and preview. Keep favorites close and restore deleted files from trash.

02 / SHARE

Send a link. Set the limits.

Share files and folders with password protection, expiry times, and QR codes.

03 / MANAGE

Make room for everyone.

Manage users, storage quotas, folder permissions, and remote storage providers.

04 / MONITOR

Know how things are running.

Follow CPU, memory, storage, network traffic, and activity from your NAS dashboard.

LESS SETUP. MORE OWNERSHIP.

One executable.
A space of your own.

Built with Go, SQLite, and Next.js. Release downloads include the interface and API, so you don’t need Go, Node.js, or a separate database server to run TryNAS.
